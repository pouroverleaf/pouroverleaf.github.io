This session we just played around with the beans we had as a nice introductory session.

## Coffee lineup

### Natural red bourbon · Burundi

**pourover, lily drip · 15 g · 1:15**

Pours · 50 g bloom, 70, 70, remaining 35

a bit underextracted. Should not use lily drip. Tastes refreshing and fruity though

### Origin · lemon & blueberry

**pourover, lily drip · 18 g · 1:16.7 (18 g to 300 g)**

Pours · 60 g bloom, 3 pours, 3 1/2 minutes

tastes like a darker roast. Harder to taste the notes, a bit lemony after taste

### Chelangat, Sipi · Uganda

Listed notes · orange, plum, milk chocolate

**espresso, Delonghi dedica · 18 g, double shot**

### Ultralight Honduras El Mango

Listed notes · peach, passionflower, ginger lily

1600–1640 m

**pourover, lilydrip · 15 g · 1:15**

super light and fruity at the end. If someone says "I dont like coffee" and you want to turn them into a coffee nerd, this is a good place to start.

### Ethiopia gesha · anaerobic natural

Listed notes · summer fruits, caramel, honeysuckle

1931–2040 m

**pourover, lily drip · 15 g**

### Indonesian · Kerinci, sigararutang

anaerobic honey

**pourover, no lily drip · 15 g · 1:15**

sour smell and taste, but pleasant, not harsh.

### Siti · Ethiopian landrace

2200 m

washed

**pourover, no lilydrip · 15 g · 1:15**

smells herbal or grasslike. Tastes smooth, not sour or intense at all
