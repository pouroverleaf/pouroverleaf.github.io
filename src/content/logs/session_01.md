This session we just played around with the beans we had as a nice introductory session.

## Natural red bourbon · Burundi

- **Brew:** pourover, lily drip
- **Dose:** 15g
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Pours:** 50g bloom, 70, 70, remaining 35
- **Experience:** a bit underextracted. Should not use lily drip. Tastes refreshing and fruity though

## Origin · lemon & blueberry

- **Brew:** pourover, lily drip
- **Dose:** 18g
- **Ratio:** 18:300
- **Pours:** 60g bloom, 3 pours, 3 1/2 minutes
- **Experience:** tastes like a darker roast. Harder to taste the notes, a bit lemony after taste

## Chelangat, Sipi · Uganda

- **Notes:** orangle, plum, milk chocolate
- **Brew:** espresso, Delonghi dedica
- **Dose:** 18g, double shot

## Ultralight Honduras El Mango

- **Notes:** peach, passionflower, ginger lily
- **MASL:** 1600–1640
- **Brew:** pourover, lilydrip
- **Dose:** 15g
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Experience:** super light and fruity at the end. If someone says "I dont like coffee" and you want to turn them into a coffee nerd, this is a good place to start.

## Ethiopia gesha · anaerobic natural

- **Notes:** summer fruits, caramel, honeysuckle
- **MASL:** 1931–2040
- **Brew:** pourover, lily drip
- **Dose:** 15g

## Indonesian · Kerinci, sigararutang

- **Process:** anaerobic honey
- **Brew:** pourover, no lily drip
- **Dose:** 15g
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Experience:** sour smell and taste, but pleasant, not harsh.

## Siti · Ethiopian landrace

- **Process:** washed
- **MASL:** 2200
- **Brew:** pourover, no lilydrip
- **Dose:** 15g
- **Ratio:** 1:15
- **Experience:** smells herbal or grasslike. Tastes smooth, not sour or intense at all
