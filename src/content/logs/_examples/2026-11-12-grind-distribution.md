Placeholder log. We sieved three grinders at the same nominal setting and measured extraction yield on a V60 at 1:16.

## Setup

- 15 g dose, 240 g water at 94 °C
- Hario Switch, open valve
- Refractometer readings averaged over three brews

## Result

Wider distributions brewed slower and tasted muddier at the same yield. The narrowest distribution hit 21% at the shortest contact time.
