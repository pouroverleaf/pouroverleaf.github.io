pouroverleaf is a special interest group under [CompSoc](https://comp-soc.com/) at the University of Edinburgh dedicated to coffee, research, and coffee research. 

founded with a mild annoyance to the vague, wishywashy terms used to describe coffees and the pseudoscience of brewing, we set off to quantitatively and qualitatively evaluate the fundamental question of<br>
**"what makes good coffee good?"**.

in our weekly sessions, we experiment with the many variables in coffeemaking to understand the factors that contribute to the quality of the cup, and discuss our ongoing research projects once highly caffeinated.

we're usually at AT4 on wednesdays 10:00-12:00.
