About

pouroverleaf is a student-run club at the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh. Half coffee club, half research club.

Coffee

Every two weeks we bring brewing gear and specialty beans. We do pourover, espresso, and tea.

Research

Undergraduates, master's students and PhD students share the research they do and the research they find interesting. Bring a paper, a project. Questions are welcome too.

Format

Lowkey. No talks schedule unless otherwise specified, no sign-up. Come for coffee & discussion.
